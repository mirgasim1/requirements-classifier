The original_only and augmented safetensors files are modified derivatives.
The changes are supervised FR/NFR fine-tuning of the pretrained encoder and
the addition of a two-output classification layer. The training procedure,
source datasets, validation choices, results and checkpoint hashes are recorded
in this experiment's protocol, source code and results files.

Original-only training uses the original 4,748-record training subset.
Augmented training adds the documented 1,496-record PROMISE+ training pool.
Neither condition uses the reserved test records for fitting or epoch selection.

the base-model publisher endorses these derivatives or their predictions.
